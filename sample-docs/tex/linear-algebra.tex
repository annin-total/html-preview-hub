\section{Scaled dot-product attention}

For queries $Q \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}$, keys $K \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times d}$ and
values $V \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times d_v}$,
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^{\top}}{\sqrt{d}}\right) V .
\end{equation}

\section{Why the scaling factor}

If the entries of $q$ and $k$ are independent with zero mean and unit variance, then
\begin{align}
  \mathbb{E}[q^{\top} k] &= 0, &
  \mathrm{Var}(q^{\top} k) &= d ,
\end{align}
so dividing by $\sqrt{d}$ keeps the logits at unit scale and prevents the softmax from
saturating as $d$ grows.
